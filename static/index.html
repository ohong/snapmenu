Menu privacy

Your photo is sent to OpenAI to read and translate it. SnapMenu does not keep the original file.

Your translated menu and dish pictures are saved for seven days and are available only to this browser. You can delete your menu at any time.

Requesting a picture sends that dish’s name and description to OpenAI. Image requests run in the background and use OpenAI’s data retention settings. Pictures are AI interpretations, not restaurant photos.

Translations can make mistakes. Check prices, ingredients, and allergies with the restaurant.
